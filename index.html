Stage 1

Briefing

Read the case background and understand your team mission.

CompanyVistaline Solutions
DepartmentOperations & Client Support Coordination
ProjectClient Experience Relay (CER)

Background. Vistaline Solutions provides integrated services to large B2B clients, including logistics, banking, and public services. After repeated complaints from high-value clients about delays and confusion post-signing, leadership introduced the Client Experience Relay (CER) initiative.

The goal was to ensure that after a contract is signed, client onboarding becomes smooth, fast, and consistent — with clear internal handoffs, ownership, and visibility. This simulation takes place six weeks after the launch of CER.

Your Mission

You are a cross-functional team asked to conduct a performance diagnosis of Project CER.

  1. Review the current process map
  2. Analyze the WBS that guided the launch
  3. Audit the OKRs
  4. Evaluate the KPI dashboard and analytics
  5. Apply control logic to decide where and how to act
  6. Define proper tolerance bands
  7. Spot signs of emerging risk
  8. Recommend corrective actions

Stage 6

Tolerance Band Design

Define realistic limits and determine when action should be triggered.

Tolerance Band Worksheet — Provided Data

KPI: Welcome calls within 48 hrs

Target: 90%
Upper Limit: 95% — above this is excellent
Lower Limit: 85% — below this triggers review
Intervention Required If: <85% for 2 consecutive weeks
Recommended Action: Review handoff SOP and auto-reminders for follow-up

KPI: Staff tool usage

Target: 95%
Upper Limit: 100%
Lower Limit: 85%
Intervention Required If: <85% or downward trend across 3 weeks
Recommended Action: Assign champions, re-train on importance of logging

Your Tasks

  • Define realistic upper/lower thresholds.
  • Decide at what point an action must be triggered.
  • Decide when to revise the KPI vs. correct the team behavior.
Example clue from the participant case: A 3-week drop in welcome call % implies instability.
